About Stroll & Steam Tours

Rooted in the rich maritime heritage of Roatán's East End, providing safe, personalized, and unforgettable coastal excursions.

Our Island Roots & Story

Born and raised amidst the winding waterways and fishing traditions of Roatán's East End, Stroll & Steam Tours offers a genuine look into island life.

We take pride in guiding travelers far beyond the crowded commercial areas, offering direct access to the hidden labyrinths of Jonesville and Oakridge mangroves, pristine barrier reefs, and historic coastal communities like Port Royal and Punta Gorda.

Whether you're stepping off a cruise ship in Coxen Hole or staying on the island, our mission is to treat every guest like family while maintaining the highest standards of safety, professionalism, and environmental respect.
