Terms & Conditions

Please read our booking policies, cancellation guidelines, and activity terms prior to reserving your East End Roatán excursion.

By booking a tour, excursion, transportation service, or marine activity with Stroll & Steam Tours, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the following Terms & Conditions.

1 Reservations & Booking Confirmation

All reservations are subject to availability. A booking is considered confirmed once the customer receives confirmation from Stroll & Steam Tours.

Guests are responsible for providing accurate information when making a reservation, including the number of participants, contact information, cruise ship details (when applicable), and any special requirements or dietary needs.

2 Payments, Down Payments & Inclusions

Prices are quoted in U.S. dollars (USD) unless otherwise stated.

To confirm and lock in a tour reservation, guests may select either a 30% down payment (deposit) at checkout or elect to pay the full amount upfront via PayPal or credit card.

If a 30% deposit is selected, the remaining 70% balance must be paid in full on or before the tour date prior to boarding, via cash or agreed payment instructions provided with your reservation confirmation.

Additional food, alcoholic beverages, souvenirs, third-party entrance fees, or special boat transfers not specifically listed as included in your chosen tour package carry an additional charge.

3 Cancellations & Refunds

We understand that travel plans can change unpredictably:

  • Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the scheduled tour time are eligible for a full refund.
  • Cancellations made less than 24 hours before departure, as well as no-shows at the designated pickup area, are non-refundable.
  • 100% Port Cancellation Refund: If your cruise ship does not dock in Roatán due to weather or port rerouting, guests receive a 100% full refund.
  • If Stroll & Steam Tours must cancel an excursion due to severe marine weather, unsafe sea conditions, or unexpected mechanical issues, guests will be offered an alternative date, a substituted experience, or a 100% full refund.
  • Same-day guest cancellations are non-refundable.

4 Cruise Ship Guests & Back-To-Ship Guarantee

We understand the critical importance of returning cruise passengers to Coxen Hole or Mahogany Bay with plenty of time to spare before ship departure.

Guests are responsible for providing their correct ship name and official port schedule upon booking. Boat tour departure times are tailored specifically around your ship's arrival and departure schedule.

If your cruise ship cancels its port call to Roatán, please notify us as soon as possible to expedite your full refund processing.

5 Marine Weather & Itinerary Changes

Roatán's coastal weather, sea swell, and marine visibility can shift quickly. Outdoor marine activities—such as barrier reef snorkeling, Pigeon Cay shark encounters, or Barbareta Jade Beach landings—may be modified, delayed, re-routed, or substituted due to wind, water safety, or sea state.

Stroll & Steam Tours reserves the right to adjust boat routes or swap open-water snorkeling spots for protected mangrove channel cruises to protect the safety and comfort of our guests.

6 Water Activities & Physical Safety

Our boat excursions feature open-water swimming, barrier reef snorkeling, mangrove canal cruising, and wildlife encounters.

Guests are responsible for assessing their physical capabilities prior to swimming or snorkeling. Guests must follow all marine safety instructions given by Captain Yusef, guide Jerick, and crew members.

Life vests are provided for all guests on board and must be worn whenever instructed by the captain or during required water activities.

7 Third-Party Venues & Stopover Attractions

Select excursions include stops at seaside restaurants (such as On the Rocks), Garifuna cultural centers in Punta Gorda, or independent beach facilities.

Stroll & Steam Tours coordinates these local stops for guest convenience but does not control the independent day-to-day operations of third-party restaurants or community sites. Guests must respect local community rules and venue policies.

8 Personal Belongings on Board

Guests are responsible for their personal items, electronic devices, bags, and gear during boat rides, transport, and snorkeling stops.

Stroll & Steam Tours is not responsible for items lost, dropped in the water, stolen, or damaged by sea spray, except where mandated by law. We strongly recommend storing phones, cameras, and valuables in waterproof bags.

9 Transportation & Port Transfers

Port pick-up and ground transport are provided in accordance with the specific tour package booked.

Guests must follow driver safety instructions while in transit across the island. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times during land and water transport.

10 Guest Conduct & Marine Safety

Guests are expected to treat captains, guides, drivers, fellow travelers, local islanders, and marine life with respect.

Stroll & Steam Tours reserves the right to refuse boarding or discontinue a tour without refund if a guest exhibits disruptive behavior, excessive intoxication, or conduct that compromises vessel safety or crew operations.

11 Children & Family Guidance

Children must remain under the direct supervision of a parent or guardian throughout all boat transit, mangrove canal rides, and snorkeling stops.

Parents are responsible for ensuring fitting life vests are secured on children prior to boat departures.

12 Reservation Changes

Requests to adjust departure times, party size, or tour packages are subject to boat availability and schedule alignment. Please contact us as early as possible via WhatsApp or email if you need to modify your booking.

13 Safety & Assumption of Risk

Ocean navigation, boating, snorkeling, and water recreation carry inherent ocean risks. By participating voluntarily, guests acknowledge ordinary risks associated with coastal boat travel and water activities.

Stroll & Steam Tours takes all standard maritime safety precautions to deliver a secure, enjoyable, and memorable East End experience.

14 Acceptance of Terms

By completing a reservation or participating in any excursion hosted by Stroll & Steam Tours, guests confirm that they have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms & Conditions.
